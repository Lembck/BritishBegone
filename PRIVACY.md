# Privacy Policy for BritishBegone

Last updated: [Current Date]

## Data Collection
BritishBegone does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. 

## How the Extension Works
The extension performs text replacement locally in your browser when you click the extension icon. All processing happens on your device, and no information is sent to external servers.

## Permissions Used
- **scripting**: Used to modify text on web pages
- **activeTab**: Used to access the current tab's content only when you activate the extension
- **storage**: Used to save your extension preferences locally on your device

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not use any third-party services or analytics.

For questions about this privacy policy, please visit: https://github.com/Lembck/BritishBegone
